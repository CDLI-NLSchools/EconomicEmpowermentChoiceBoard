Create a Comic Strip

A comic strip tells a story with graphics, captions and speech in one or multiple frames.

Create a comic strip with an original character (or your pet rock) to tell a story on the concepts outlined in the Extension Activities video.

When planning your comic strip, consider the following elements: Colour; Speech or thought bubbles; Graphics; Captions.

Plan your comic strip

An original character, or your pet rock. Give your character a name.

What happens in your story, from the beginning to the end?

How will you use colour in your strip?

Choose a template

Frame by frame

Plan each frame of your strip. You can copy bubble shapes and graphics into your frames when you draw the real thing.

Create a Digital Poster

A poster combines words and visuals to deliver information to an audience on an important topic. Using different color fonts, backgrounds, along with a variety of shapes and images can create mood and show relationships between topics and ideas.

When planning for your poster, consider the following design elements:

Plan your poster

The important topic your poster will deliver information about.

Who is your poster for?

The big title at the top of your poster.

The explanatory text your poster will carry. Add one point per row, and add as many rows as you need.

My design plan

Use a variety of font styles and sizes to highlight important information. How will you use fonts?

Use a variety of shapes, drawings, pictures, etc. to help convey the information. What visuals will you use?

Use a variety of colors that suit the mood of your poster. What colors will you use, and what mood will they set?

It is important that your poster follows a logical order, so it is easy for the audience to understand. How will you organize your poster?

Create a Photo Essay of Your Pet Rock

A photo essay uses photos and captions to display knowledge of a particular subject.

Take your pet rock and go for a walk. Photograph them with the particulars outlined in the Extension Activities video, or find photos online and put your pet rock in them. Then create a photo essay.

When planning your photo essay, consider the following: Photos; Captions to display the information from your pet rock's point of view; Shapes for attention and balance; A title; White Space (which can actually be any colour).

Plan your photo essay

Give your photo essay a title.

My design plan

What shapes will you place around your photos to catch the eye and balance the page?

White space can actually be any colour. What background colour will you leave around your photos?
