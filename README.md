# Explore the Land Choice Board

A single-file choice board activity for Grade 7 STEAM, Explore the Land, Lesson 7 Extension: Economic Empowerment. It replaces a Jamboard extension activity that no longer works.

Following the original board, students choose ONE of three extension activities and plan it here:

1. Create a Comic Strip: name a main character (an original character, or their pet rock), outline the story, plan the colour, choose one of the board's four panel templates (6, 6, 5, or 5 frames), and plan each frame's caption, speech or thought bubble, and graphics.
2. Create a Digital Poster: set a topic, audience, and heading, list the information the poster will carry, and plan the design using the board's four elements (Fonts, Visuals, Color, Organization).
3. Create a Photo Essay of Your Pet Rock: title the essay, plan each photo and a caption written from the pet rock's point of view, and plan shapes and white space.

A summary compiles the chosen activity and the student's plan, with Print and Copy buttons, so students can keep it or paste it into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
